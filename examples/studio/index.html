ORP v0.2 · Optical Relay Protocol

Bytes carried by light.

OptiRelay moves files from one device to another through a screen and a camera: no network, no Bluetooth, no cable. Authenticated pairing, AES-256-GCM, Reed-Solomon, fountain code and temporal colour modulation.

  • @optirelay/core
  • @optirelay/optical
  • @optirelay/browser

The whole protocol runs here, on a single device: the sender paints real ORP frames, a simulated optical channel distorts them the way a hand-held camera would (degradeImage), and the receiver decodes them pixel by pixel (TmReceiver). Degrade the channel and watch what breaks, what Reed-Solomon repairs and what the fountain code catches up on.

        The block is split into K source symbols. Each symbol sent is the XOR of a small subset drawn from a seed (robust soliton). It does not matter which ones get lost: receiving slightly more than K is enough. The ORP decoder combines peeling and Gaussian elimination over GF(2).
