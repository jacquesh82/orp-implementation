Subframe duration

Live, the phone must analyse at least one image per subframe: ≈ 100 ms for 1 grid, 150 ms or more for 3 × 3. 17 and 33 ms (one or two screen refreshes): only when filmed in slow motion (240 fps), then “Import a video”.

Mosaic

Several frames per screen: throughput multiplied, but smaller modules — bring the phone closer. 4 × 4 and beyond: 4K receiver.

Start delay

Time to pick up the phone: the screen shows the initialization code during the countdown.

Display

The new window can go to another screen, the one you film. It switches to full screen by itself or on the first click in it (F key); closing it stops the sending.

Estimate: choose a file

Receive through the camera

The phone films the transmission in slow motion (240 fps in the APK), then reads every image offline: 17 or 33 ms subframes become possible.

Waiting for a transfer.

On the other device: “Transfer test” tab, choose a file, “Send”. You can aim at any time: the initialization code comes back regularly.

A missed frame is never resent: each frame carries a fresh combination of the file's blocks (fountain code), and the next ones replace it. Receiving slightly more frames than there are blocks is enough, whichever they are.
